Private Car Parity Note

For private cars and GCV (7.5T-40T) older than 10 years, the revised OD discount remains identical whether the client opts for Add-on covers or standard cover (80% / 78% for cars, 75% / 70% for commercial).

Heavy Commercial (>40T) Renewal

GCV (>40,000 GVW) renewals carry an exclusive loyalty differential: Existing NIA policyholders receive 5% to 10% higher discount compared to other company rollovers across all vehicle age brackets.

SAOD Compliance

Standalone OD (SAOD) can only be underwritten when a valid long-term Third Party (TP) policy is active. Claim-free expiring records qualify for 82% OD discount; otherwise 78% applies.
